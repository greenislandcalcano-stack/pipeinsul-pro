✂ Segmented Elbow Pattern Generator · V3.0 BETA

Flat developments for cylindrical, straight-mitered elbow jacket sections. Preview the first, middle or last segment and export an SVG with physical millimeter dimensions. Not for pressed shells or compound-curved gores.

Fabrication caution: Geometric reference for ideal circular pipe and equal-deflection miter joints. The centerline radius is approximated by a polygonal miter centerline. No circumferential seam allowances, hems, crimping, bead, clearance, metal thickness, insulation compression, field tolerance, or manufacturer specifications are included. SVG is dimensioned in millimeters; verify the calibration bar after printing at 100% / actual size. Do not cut production material until the pattern is verified on a physical mock-up.
