📐 Calculated Measurements

Cut width = π × finished OD + side overlap. Jacket quantity uses stock length minus end lap as effective coverage (first piece has full stock length). Surface area is finished cylinder area, excluding end faces. Insulation volume is annular volume. Does not include fittings, supports, vapor barriers, seams, corrugation allowances, sheet nesting, or project-specific specifications.
